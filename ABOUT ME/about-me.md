**Obligations and timeline**
| Article | Obligation | Applies from | A.R.E.N.A. action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art. 6 + Annex I | Substance restrictions: Hg ≤0.0005 %, Cd ≤0.002 % (portable), Pb ≤0.01 % (portable) | Hg/Cd in force; Pb 18 Aug 2024 | Lab test per cell/pack; REACH/RoHS cross-check |
| Art. 17–20 | Conformity assessment (Module A), EU DoC, technical documentation, CE marking | 18 Aug 2024 | DoC + TCF review at Link 6–7; CE on battery |
| Art. 13(1)–(3) | General label, capacity label (rechargeable portable/LMT/SLI), minimum-duration label (non-rechargeable portable) | 18 Aug 2026 or 18 months after labelling implementing act, whichever is later | Artwork check: pending implementing act; monitor |
| Art. 13(4) | Separate-collection (crossed-out bin) symbol; Cd/Pb chemical symbols where thresholds exceeded | 18 Aug 2025 | Artwork/packaging gate |
| Art. 13(6) | QR code giving access to label information | 18 Feb 2027 (sources consistent; EUR-Lex text check pending) | Supplier data request |
| Art. 11 | Portable batteries removable and replaceable by end-user; LMT by independent professionals | 18 Feb 2027 | Design review at RFx / QSD stage: **critical for GARDEN/DIY/DECO sealed products** |
| Art. 47–53 | Supply-chain due diligence (Co, Li, Ni, natural graphite): only economic operators with net turnover ≥ €40 M | 18 Aug 2027 (postponed by (EU) 2025/1561) | Ally-level; A.R.E.N.A. supplies smelter/refiner data |
| Art. 55–62 | Extended producer responsibility: producer registration in each Member State, take-back | 18 Aug 2025 | Ally as producer; A.R.E.N.A. supplies weight/chemistry data |
| Art. 77–78 | Digital battery passport: LMT, industrial >2 kWh, EV | 18 Feb 2027 | Applies to e-bike, ESS, large garden packs |
| Art. 7 / Art. 8 | Carbon footprint declaration (EV, rechargeable industrial >2 kWh, LMT) / recycled content documentation | Phased; depends on delegated acts | Watch list |
| Art. 9–10 | Performance and durability: rechargeable industrial, LMT, EV / portable general use | Phased by delegated acts | Watch list |

**Non-EU markets**
- **Switzerland:** ChemRRV Annex 2.15 (batteries); aligns closely with the EU; VREG take-back.
- **Canada:** provincial battery EPR schemes (e.g. Call2Recycle); CCPSA for consumer products; button/coin battery warnings.
- **US:** Reese's Law / 16 CFR 1263 (button/coin cell products); CPSC; state EPR (e.g. CA, VT, WA, CO, IL).
- **Transport (all markets):** UN 38.3 test summary + MSDS for every lithium cell/pack; IATA/IMDG packing instructions.

**Standard evidence package (per battery SKU)**
UN 38.3 test summary · IEC 62133-2 (Li) / IEC 62133-1 (Ni) · substance test (Hg/Cd/Pb) · EU DoC (Battery Reg.) · label artwork · MSDS · cell supplier ID · capacity/chemistry/weight declaration · removability statement (Art. 11).
